TECH SCREEN

Press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Enter or click Solve to capture your screen and ask the model. The overlay auto-hides during capture so it isn't in the screenshot, and (where supported) is invisible to screen-sharing tools.

Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+M toggles click-through so you can scroll/select inside this panel. Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Space shows/hides the overlay.
